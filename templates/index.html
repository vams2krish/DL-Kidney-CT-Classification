UNDER THE HOOD

How classification works

01 / PREPARE

Resize & normalize

Convert to RGB, resize to 224 × 224, and rescale pixels to match training.

02 / INFER

Frozen VGG16

An ImageNet backbone extracts features. A two-class head returns model scores.

03 / INSPECT

Understand the limits

Compare raw scores. Neither the label nor its score establishes a diagnosis.
